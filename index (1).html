About

Capturing the southern sky above Tekapo

I am Merlin Williams, an astrophotographer based in Lake Tekapo, New Zealand — inside the Aoraki Mackenzie International Dark Sky Reserve, one of the darkest and most protected skies on Earth.

Originally from Melbourne, I made Tekapo my permanent base to pursue deep-sky imaging of nebulae, galaxies, and star-forming regions visible only from the southern hemisphere. My work uses narrowband and broadband techniques across long multi-night integration sessions.

Available for fine art prints, editorial licensing, and guided dark-sky sessions for visitors to the Mackenzie Basin.

The Dark Sky Reserve

Aoraki Mackenzie

Lake Tekapo sits within one of the world's largest dark sky preserves. At 760 m altitude with over 200 clear nights per year and minimal light pollution, it is among the finest sites on Earth for southern hemisphere astrophotography — offering targets invisible from the northern hemisphere, including the Magellanic Clouds and the heart of the Milky Way.
